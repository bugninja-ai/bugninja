## FAQ

### Can I try Bugninja before purchasing a commercial license?

Yes. You can evaluate Bugninja for testing and development purposes. However, production use in a commercial environment requires a license.

### Do I need a license for open-source projects?

If your project is genuinely noncommercial and open-source, the Polyform Noncommercial license covers you. If your open-source project is backed by a commercial entity or generates revenue, please contact us.

### What about contractors working for my company?

Contractors working on behalf of your company fall under your company's license. If your company requires a commercial license, that covers contractor work done for you.
